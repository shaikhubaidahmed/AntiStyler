# YOLOv7 AntiStyler Defense Experiment

## Experiment Details
- **Dataset**: GTSDB (Test Images: 54, GT Annotations: 82)
- **Detector**: YOLOv7
- **YOLOv7 Commit**: `a207844`
- **Checkpoint**: `experiments/yolov7_gtsdb/run/weights/best.pt`
- **Checkpoint SHA256**: `fdc2e7a23f33ab3639fb325cd84ab405a477233dfad72e59358dcd91941aabb6`

## Attack Profile
- **Attack**: Corrected DPatch (Spatially localized objective)
- **Resolution**: 416x416

## AntiStyler Configuration
- **AntiStyler Version**: Validated Implementation
- **Feature Extractor**: Pretrained VGG19
- **Style Layers**: `relu1_1`, `relu2_1`, `relu3_1`, `relu4_1`, `relu5_1`
- **Content Layer**: `relu4_2`
- **Optimization Steps**: 1
- **Alpha**: 1
- **Beta**: 1000
- **Padding**: 10
- **Hardware**: NVIDIA Quadro RTX 4000

## Quantitative Results Comparison

| Condition | COCO mAP@0.50 | Precision | Recall | mAP@0.50:0.95 | AP75 | Detections |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|--------|----------------|------|------------|
| Clean | 0.316 | 0.416 | 0.441 | 0.201 | N/A | 12,525 |
| Attacked | 0.077 | 0.127 | 0.144 | 0.046 | 0.057 | 16,200 |
| Defended | 0.234 | 0.455 | 0.265 | 0.147 | 0.171 | 15,633 |

## Defense Performance Summary

- **Clean mAP@0.50**: 0.316
- **Attacked mAP@0.50**: 0.077
- **Defended mAP@0.50**: 0.234

- **Attack Degradation**: 0.239 (mAP lost due to DPatch)
- **Defense Recovery**: 0.157 (mAP regained by AntiStyler)
- **Recovery Percentage**: 65.69% of the lost performance was successfully recovered.
- **Defended Performance Relative to Clean**: The defended model operates at 74.05% of the original clean baseline capacity.

## Detection Quality Analysis
The DPatch attack flooded the detection pipeline with false positives, maxing out the detections at 16,200. When AntiStyler was applied:
- The YOLOv7 precision surged from 0.127 (attacked) to 0.455 (defended), even surpassing the clean baseline precision (0.416). This strongly indicates that AntiStyler effectively neutralized the highly-confident false positive hallucination (class ANIMALS) generated by the adversarial patch.
- Defended recall (0.265) improved significantly from the attacked state (0.144), meaning true traffic signs previously suppressed by the patch were successfully restored. 

## Timing Analysis
- **Mean AntiStyler time/image**: 125.57 ms
- **Mean YOLOv7 inference time/image**: 16.9 ms (Inference + NMS)
- **Mean total defense pipeline time/image**: 142.47 ms
- **Defense Pipeline Throughput**: ~7.02 FPS

## Conclusion
The first end-to-end AntiStyler defense experiment on YOLOv7 was a success. Without any parameter tuning or optimization, the zero-shot application of the single-step AntiStyler formulation recovered 65.69% of the mAP lost to the DPatch attack, restoring YOLOv7's capability to detect small traffic signs in the presence of an aggressive localized patch.
